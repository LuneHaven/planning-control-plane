{# Status shapes as drawn SVG, not text glyphs (IDEA-0003). Why drawn: measured on the real 11px badge, the geometric characters this replaces are drawn to incompatible sizes by the fonts the offline stack falls back to — circles at 8x7px, squares and triangles at 10x10, U+25D4 at 8x5 and unreadable — and every font resolves them differently, so the shape dimension carried no information. One viewBox and one stroke width put all of them on the same visual weight, on every platform, with no font dependency. Why inlined per page: `` is blocked by the same-origin policy under file://, where these pages are meant to open (measured: external bbox 0, inline bbox 27). The sprite is small and costs no network request, so every page carries its own copy. #} {% macro sprite() -%} {%- endmacro %} {# One status/track shape. Decorative by definition: the badge always carries the localized text label next to it, so the shape is never the only way to read a status. #} {% macro shape(id, extra_class="") -%} {%- endmacro %}
